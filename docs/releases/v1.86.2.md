# AIHub Browser 1.86.2

## AIHub DJ
- **Original music only:** the AI DJ plays only original recordings by the real artists. It skips AI covers, AI-generated songs, and uploads from Suno, Udio or channels that label themselves AI music. This applies to set planning, the stage search, model picks, spoken requests and "play X" requests.
- **No volume jump on first play:** streamed songs no longer get louder by themselves about 24 seconds in. Auto-gain now levels a deck only while it can't be heard: when you're cueing in headphones, or its fader or crossfader side is closed.
- **Technics tonearm:** every turntable skin has a Technics-style arm. It has a black base, a counterweight, a straight chrome tube and a headshell, and when no song is loaded it rests beside the record, off the platter. Loading a song swings it onto the record, and it tracks inwards as the song plays.
- **Deck colours:** the stylus target light glows red on deck B and blue on deck A.
