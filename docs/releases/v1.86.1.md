# AIHub Browser 1.86.1

This release includes the 1.86.0 changes.

## AIHub DJ skins
- Six hardware-replica skins, each with a large platter: **Technics Silver**, **Technics Silver 4**, **Classic 1200**, **Battle Artwork**, **Battle Black** (new) and **Ember Vinyl**. They include live jog LCDs, an S-shaped tonearm, a red power cap, mixer displays and a START·STOP key.
- Every turntable skin now has the same turntable details: a 45 adapter, a stylus target light that lights up when a record is loaded, an arm board, a playing lamp and a green pitch LCD.
- The rig skins use Classic text sizes for track titles, clocks, readouts and the browser.
- Cover-flow thumbnails appear in every skin again.
- Colour palettes repaint the rig skins. Choose "Skin original" to restore each rig's own finish.

## Sampler sounds
- Every pad sound has been rebuilt to sound like the real thing: a dancehall air horn, a police wail siren, a "pew-pew" laser, a riser that builds from the first beat, a cinematic impact, a hand clap with room, a spin-back rewind and a tom fill.
- Fixes pads that were silent: CLAP was too quiet, and HIT and TOM were inaudible on laptop speakers. The horn no longer clips.

## Fixes
- The red "AI DJ" tab in the side panel stays on one line.
