# AIHub Browser 1.88.4

## Bible
- **The natural voice is now on by default:** you no longer have to find and install it. The first time you open the Bible or Bible Study, the human-sounding voice downloads by itself in the background (about 165 MB, once). Until 1.88.4 it had to be installed from Settings → Bible, so most people kept hearing the robotic Windows voice.
- **No more robotic voice while it downloads:** press Listen before the download finishes and the button shows "Preparing voice 34%", then reads with the natural voice.
- **Steadier:** if the first sentence fails once, the voice tries again instead of switching to the robotic one.
- Removing the voice in Settings → Bible keeps it off; it won't download again unless you press Install.
