# AIHub Browser 1.87.0

## AIHub DJ
- **NEXT tab — song suggestions:** while a song plays, the NEXT tab offers three songs from your library to mix into next. Each one has a reason, three hot cues and a loop on its phrase grid, and buttons to load it on a deck or mix it in. The cues and loop go straight onto the deck's pads. Suggestions come from Ollama running on your computer, with your AI fallback setting applied when it can't answer.
- **Matches tempo and key:** suggestions stay within 6 % of the playing tempo (half and double time count) and in a key that mixes on the Camelot wheel. Turn on Pivot keys for bolder key changes.
- **Reads the floor:** set the floor energy with the slider (or from an external meter) and the order of suggestions follows it, stepping the set up or cooling it down.
- **Mashups, FX and stems:** layer a suggested pair on decks C and D, arm an FX macro timed to the playing song's breakdown, and let Auto stems clear clashing vocals or basslines during a blend.
- **Voice filter:** hold the talk button and say what you want ("only 90s", "faster", an artist). This needs a speech-to-text server such as whisper.cpp running on your computer.
- **Earlier suggestions:** Prev and Next page back through earlier suggestion sets.
- **Your library builds itself:** songs are added as you browse folders and play them; genre and year are now read from MP3 tags. Private windows never add to it.
- **Wider side panel:** the set list, queue, AI DJ and NEXT tabs all fit without being cut off.
