# AIHub Browser 1.89.0

## Bible
- **One language at a time:** the Bible version you pick now sets the language of everything around it. Choose Louis Segond and the whole Bible is in French — the reader's buttons, the Study rooms, the monthly reading, reading plans, badges, the Gospel room, the voice settings and the study assistant. Choose the World English Bible and it is all English. No more English buttons around French scripture.
- **All 24 courses in French:** every lesson in the Classroom — 124 lessons of teaching — has been translated, along with the monthly readings, reading plans and badges. The passages and memory verses stay the same; they are read from the version you have open.
- **French where it matters:** book names, chapter labels ("Psaume 23", "Jour 3"), dates ("1 octobre") and the AI study companion's suggestions follow the version too. The Gospel room offers French worship in place of Spanish when you read in French.
