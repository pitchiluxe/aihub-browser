# AIHub Browser 1.86.0

- Rebuilds the AIHub DJ reference skins as hardware replicas, each one closer to the rig it is based on:
  - **Technics Silver** and **Technics Silver 4**: machined silver jogs with a dimpled rim and a blue hub LCD that shows elapsed time and BPM as the track plays. Technics Silver also gets an amber mixer display.
  - **Classic 1200**: an aluminium plinth with black vinyl, a green label on deck A, a red label on deck B and an S-shaped tonearm.
  - **Battle Artwork** and the new **Battle Black**: black battle decks with a red power cap on the record and a red LED mixer display.
  - **Ember Vinyl**: orange vinyl on a black plinth, with a blue LCD on the mixer.
- Gives the record more of the deck in these skins. The transport moves into a column with a START·STOP key, and the track strips now sit under the decks.
- Colour palettes repaint the rig skins. Choose "Skin original" to get each rig's own finish back. Records, LCDs and lamps keep their hardware colours either way.
- Fixes the red "AI DJ" tab in the browser side panel. It used to wrap onto two lines and stick out of the header.
- Track artwork still fills the whole record, and the other skins are unchanged.
