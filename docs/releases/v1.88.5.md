# AIHub Browser 1.88.5

## Bible
- **The natural voice now reads French:** Louis Segond (and the French Kids Bible) is read by a natural French voice instead of the robotic English Windows voice — most Windows PCs have no French voice at all, so French scripture used to come out in an English accent. References are announced the French way ("Jean 3, verset 16 à 18").
- **The voice you pick is the voice you hear:** choosing a voice in Settings → Bible makes it the reading voice for every Listen button, in the reader, the Study room and the popups. The French voice is listed too and always reads Louis Segond.
