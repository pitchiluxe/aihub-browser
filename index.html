The AI Agent

Not a chatbot in a sidebar.
An agent that acts.

Press Ctrl + Shift + A and ask in plain English. The agent picks its own tools — searching the web, reading and driving your tabs, filling forms, writing files — and shows which brain is answering: your private local model through Ollama or a cloud model through OpenRouter.

🔎

Researches the live web

Searches the real internet, opens the best sources, reads them and answers with citations. It never has to say "I can't browse".

"What changed in the EU AI Act this month?"
🧭

Drives the browser

Opens, switches and closes tabs, reads any page, maps every form field, types into inputs and clicks buttons for you.

"Fill this application with my details."
🛠️

Writes & builds

Generates code, documents and whole working projects in a folder you choose, then installs, runs and fixes them until they pass.

"Build me a landing page for my bakery."
📂

Works with your files

Reads local documents (including Word files), rewrites them and saves the result — only in the folders you point it at.

"Tighten this cover letter."
🧠

Remembers what you read

Searches your private Rewind archive by meaning, keeps the conversation between launches and keeps a separate memory per site.

"That article about pricing I read yesterday?"
📊

Research & Agent Modes

Cross-reference several tabs, spot contradictions and export a cited report — or save named agents (research, shopping, code) that keep their own context.

"Compare these six tabs in one table."
🛡️
Safe by design. Nothing runs on your computer without an explicit click, file access stays inside your home folder, and text hidden in web pages or files is treated as data — it can't hijack the agent. With Ollama, nothing you type ever leaves your machine.

Questions

Frequently asked
questions.

Is AIHub Browser really free?

Yes. AIHub Browser is free to download and use on Windows, macOS and Linux, with no account and no subscription. Cloud AI models through OpenRouter use your own key; local AI through Ollama costs nothing.

Can I use AI without sending my data to the cloud?

Yes. Install Ollama and AIHub runs the AI on your own computer, so nothing you type leaves your machine. You can switch to cloud models through OpenRouter whenever you want.

What can the AI agent do?

It searches the live web and cites sources, opens and reads tabs, fills forms and clicks buttons, reads and writes files in folders you choose, and builds working projects. Any shell command needs your explicit approval, and text inside web pages cannot give it orders.

Is AIHub Browser compatible with Chrome websites?

Yes. It is built on Chromium, so every site that works in Chrome works in AIHub, including Google sign-in.

What is AIHub DJ?

AIHub DJ is a DJ console built into the browser: up to four decks, your own music and YouTube, EQ, effects, stems, real scratching, a video monitor, Automix, a YouTube queue and an AI DJ that learns your taste. It has six layouts, five looks and eighteen colour themes.

Which systems does AIHub Browser run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit), macOS 11 or later on Intel and Apple Silicon, and Linux as an AppImage. Installed copies update themselves on Windows and Linux.
